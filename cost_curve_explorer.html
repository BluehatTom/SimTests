Model used in this lab

AVC = a − bQ + cQ²

ATC = Fixed Cost ÷ Q + AVC

MC = a − 2bQ + 3cQ²

The numbers are illustrative. The starting values are chosen so the three curves are easy to distinguish while preserving their economic relationships.

Why do the intersections matter?

MC below an average → the average falls. Think of a student whose next test score is below their current average.

MC above an average → the average rises. A new value above the average pulls the average upward.

MC = AVC at minimum AVC. That is where AVC stops falling and starts rising.

MC = ATC at minimum ATC. That is where ATC stops falling and starts rising.

Fixed cost does not affect MC or AVC. Raise rent and ATC shifts, but MC and AVC stay put.
