Short-run shutdown rule

Price ≥ AVC at Q*: operate.

Price < AVC at Q*: shut down.

ATC determines profit or loss. AVC determines whether the firm should continue producing in the short run.

Why would a firm operate while losing money?

Fixed costs must be paid in the short run whether the firm produces or not. If price covers all variable costs and contributes something toward fixed costs, producing reduces the firm's loss compared with shutting down immediately.

Price > ATC
The firm earns an economic profit.
AVC ≤ Price < ATC
The firm has a loss, but continues operating in the short run.
Price < AVC
Revenue cannot cover variable costs, so the firm shuts down.
