User Testing Simulator

A branching practice lab for interviewing users and following evidence.

Enter the simulation

You are part of a design team testing an early idea with real users in mind. Your job is to learn from a stakeholder—not to sell the idea, defend it, or get approval.

What you are practicing

  • Asking open, behavioral, and experience-based questions.
  • Following up on what a user actually says instead of moving through a fixed script.
  • Recognizing when a question is leading, hypothetical, overly broad, or too solution-focused.
  • Collecting evidence before deciding what to change in a design.

How the simulator works

Each question changes the conversation. Strong questions often reveal new details and unlock better follow-up questions. Weaker questions may produce vague agreement, assumptions, or limited evidence—but you may still be able to recover the interview with a better follow-up.

You do not need to find one perfect path. The goal is to notice what different questions produce and decide when you have enough evidence to stop.

Interview reminder: Ask about what people did, noticed, expected, felt, or struggled with before asking them to judge your solution.
