ECO 201 Interactive Microeconomics Toolkit

A single launch point for the interactive tools used throughout the course.

Recommended learning path: Supply & Demand → Production → Costs → Total Revenue/Total Cost/Profit → Profit Maximization → Profit, Break-Even & Loss.
1

Supply & Demand Explorer

Market equilibrium, curve shifts, equations, shortages/surpluses, and elasticity.

2

Pizza Shop Production Function Lab

Production functions, short run vs. long run, TP, MP, AP, diminishing returns, and cost curves.

3

Cost Curve Explorer

Explore ATC, AVC, and MC, including why MC crosses the averages at their minimum points.

4

Total Cost, Revenue & Profit

Connect total cost and total revenue to profit across different output levels.

5

Profit Maximization

See how a competitive firm chooses output using the MR = MC decision rule.

6

Profit, Break-Even & Loss

Determine whether the firm earns profit, breaks even, or incurs a loss at its optimal output.

DT

User Testing Simulator

A separate design-thinking practice tool for interviewing users and following evidence.

Each tool is embedded in this file. No separate folders or web paths are required.
