ECO 201 Microeconomics Interactive Tools

Explore markets, production, costs, revenue, and firm decisions with interactive graphs and simulations. The tools are arranged in a recommended learning sequence.

Recommended learning path

Start with how markets determine price and quantity, then move inside the firm: production, costs, revenue, and profit-maximizing decisions.

1. Supply & Demand 2. Production 3. Cost Curves 4. Cost, Revenue & Profit 5. Profit Maximization 6. Profit or Loss

Microeconomics tools

1

Supply & Demand Explorer

Begin with the market. Explore curve shifts, equilibrium, shortages and surpluses, equations, and elasticity.

  • Supply and demand shifters
  • Market equilibrium
  • S/D equations and calculations
  • Elasticity
Open Supply & Demand Explorer
2

Pizza Shop Production Function Lab

Move from the market to the firm. See how labor, capital, materials, technology, and organization affect output.

  • Production function
  • Total, marginal, and average product
  • Diminishing marginal returns
  • Short run and long run
Open Production Function Lab
3

Cost Curve Explorer

Connect production decisions to costs and see why marginal cost intersects average cost curves at their minimum points.

  • ATC, AVC, and MC
  • Fixed vs. variable costs
  • Curve intersections
  • Changes in cost conditions
Open Cost Curve Explorer
4

Total Cost, Revenue & Profit

Compare total cost and total revenue across output levels and connect their difference to the firm's profit.

  • Total cost
  • Total revenue
  • Profit
  • How output changes financial results
Open Cost, Revenue & Profit Tool
5

Profit Maximization: Market Price, Costs & Output

Use the competitive firm's core decision rule: choose the output where marginal revenue is approximately equal to marginal cost.

  • Market price and MR
  • Marginal cost
  • MR = MC decision rule
  • Optimal quantity
Open Profit Maximization Tool
6

Profit, Break-Even & Loss Explorer

After finding the profit-maximizing quantity, compare price with average cost to determine whether the firm earns profit, breaks even, or incurs a loss.

  • Profit-maximizing output
  • Price vs. average cost
  • Economic profit
  • Break-even and loss
Open Profit / Break-Even / Loss Explorer
