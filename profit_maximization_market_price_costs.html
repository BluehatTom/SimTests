Quick scenarios

What should happen?

Higher market price: MR moves up → firm usually produces more.

Lower market price: MR moves down → firm usually produces less.

Higher costs: MC shifts up → firm usually produces less.

Lower costs: MC shifts down → firm usually produces more.

The decision rule

The firm compares the marginal revenue from the next unit with the marginal cost of producing it.

MR > MC
Producing another unit adds more revenue than cost → increase output.
MR ≈ MC
This is the profit-maximizing neighborhood.
MC > MR
The next unit costs more than it adds in revenue → do not expand output.
